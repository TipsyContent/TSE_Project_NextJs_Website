## Mission snapshot

- Task groups held formation for 27 hours; no warp-core variances over 0.03%.
- Subspace comms remained stable even under synchronized holo traffic.
- Delta shift cadets successfully completed live drills without manual overrides.

## Lessons learned

1. **Redundant channels matter.** Mirroring fleet commands over quantum and subspace reduced latency spikes.
2. **Human override drills.** Bridge crews that rehearsed “manual helm” transitions recovered seconds faster in simulations.
3. **Holo load balancing.** Staggering holodeck scenarios prevented power draw warnings on auxiliary relays.

## Next steps

Refit cycles will prioritize EPS harmonics and fresh diagnostics on fleetwide LCARS patches before the next maneuver exercise.
