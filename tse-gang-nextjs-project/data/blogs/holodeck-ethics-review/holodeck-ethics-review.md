Holodecks make training and recreation effortless, but they blur lines quickly.

#### Set the boundaries

- Log who can access programs; visibility keeps usage healthy.
- Avoid recreating real people without consent—memory is not a license.
- Disable physical feedback beyond training parameters unless medically cleared.

#### Keep the interlocks on

- Safety interlocks exist to prevent microfractures and neural stress.
- If a scenario demands “realistic” pain, pause and reassess the training goal.

#### Debrief every session

- Capture learnings like any holodeck drill: what changed, what failed, what felt off.
- For therapeutic runs, notify medical; for tactical runs, notify security.

Holodecks are tools, not shortcuts. Use them to prepare crews, not to replace real conversations or chain of command.
